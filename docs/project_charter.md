# 📜 Blipto: Project Charter

## 1. Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Blipto (Decentralized Hyper-Local Quick-Commerce Platform)
- **Version:** 1.0.0-alpha
- **Target Platform:** Android (Mobile Client & Store Terminal) & Realtime Microservices
- **Lead Framework:** Living Pipeline Orchestrator (LPO) Full-Lifecycle Engine

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Incumbent quick-commerce platforms (Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart) burn billions in venture capital leasing centralized dark stores, purchasing volatile perishable inventory, and subsidizing dedicated delivery fleets. Consequently, they impose aggressive hidden handling fees, surge pricing, and high take-rates (18-25%) that cannibalize traditional local mom-and-pop stores (*kiranas*). 

Meanwhile, local merchants already possess:
1. Micro-warehouses located directly inside residential clusters (0–2km).
2. Trusted relationships and freshest daily produce.
3. On-roll delivery boys currently under-utilized between peak retail hours.

**Blipto bridges this divide as a pure software coordination network.**

---

## 3. High-Level Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)

- **Objective 1: Rapid Fulfillment with Zero Dark Store CapEx**
  - *KR 1.1:* 90% of deliveries completed within $\le 20\text{ minutes}$ across a 1.5 km catchment radius.
  - *KR 1.2:* $0 platform investment in warehousing or leased physical real estate.
- **Objective 2: Zero Phantom Stockouts**
  - *KR 2.1:* Inventory mismatch cancellation rate kept below $0.5\%$ via real-time barcode intake and safety buffering.
- **Objective 3: Merchant & Consumer Trust**
  - *KR 3.1:* 100% transparent pricing (zero surge pricing, zero dark-pattern convenience fees).
  - *KR 3.2:* Instant escrow settlement to merchant upon OTP / photo verification.
  - *KR 3.3:* Automated dispute resolution with $\le 5\text{-minute}$ customer turnaround for photo-verified claims.

## 4. Scope Boundaries

| In Scope (v1.0.0 MVP) | Out of Scope (Future Phases) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Single Unified Android App with Consumer Mode, Merchant Hub, and Staff Delivery views | Centralized dark-store leasing or owned delivery fleet |
| Barcode scanning for fast inventory intake & catalog updates | Pan-country interstate multi-day logistics |
| Real-time geo-radius store discovery (500m – 2km) | Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) / high-risk unsecured lending |
| Dynamic Peak-Load Spillover routing when store capacity reaches 100% or Busy Mode is active | Warehouse automation robotics |
| Digital Escrow holding and OTP/photo delivery verification | Complex cold-chain monitoring IoT sensors |
| Instant automated photo-based dispute and refund workflow | Voice-activated ordering (v2 roadmap) |
